# KnobJot feedback

This public repository is where people who use **KnobJot** tell us what is broken, what they would like, and what they
need help with. Nothing here is the app's code.

- **Report a problem**, **Suggest an improvement**, **Ask a question**: open a new issue and pick the form. The app's
  *About → Feedback & support* box opens these forms for you with the technical details filled in.
- **Email**: `support@widgemo.com` for anything you would rather not post publicly.

## What to leave out
Issues are public. Please do not paste passwords, client names you are not free to share, or other people's contact details.
Technical details the app adds (version, page, browser, screen size) contain none of your studio data.

## For maintainers
`FEEDBACK.md` lists the exact links the app builds, so the templates and the app stay in step.
